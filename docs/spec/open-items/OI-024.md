# OI-024: HRM errors found during the spec work

## Question

Should the HRM errors be reported to the author of the HRM?

## Context

The spec work found these errors: HRM 6.5 gives `HPOSP0-3` at `$D400-$D403` (the register map gives `$D000`). HRM 14.3 gives the wide playfield as `$22-$DD` (HRM 4.3 and 6.2 give `$2C-$DD`). HRM 3.5 lists `$4C` as a NOP and leaves out `$3A`, `$5C`, `$C2`, `$E2`. HRM 3.2 says "exceeds 10" where its example shows "exceeds 9". The note under HRM Figure 6 says "cycle 100" for the `VCOUNT` change (cycle 110 or 111 elsewhere). HRM 4.12 (Abnormal DMA patterns across scan lines) gives a table for an `HSCROL` change from `$00` to `$04` that an 8-stage DMA clock cannot produce: the two bits are 2 positions apart in the table, but 4 phases apart in the model. The table agrees with the model for a change to `$08`, and the `$02` example in the same section agrees with the model (SLICE-02, REQ-345).

Second sources (OI-041) found more HRM errors: HRM 14.5 PBCTL bits 1-0 say "CA1 (SIO interrupt line)" where the table header gives CB1. HRM 9.1 gives the ACK-to-data delay as "10-18ms"; its Figure 18, its change log and SIO-810 `APP-A/Bus-timing` give 1.0-1.8 ms. HRM 7.2 says "1 is fully counterclockwise (left) and 228 is fully clockwise (right)"; HRM 5.9, TRN `HW-II.E/Paddles` and POKEY-SPEC 4 say the opposite. HRM 3.5 gives SAX as `$87, 8F, 97, 9F` and ANC as `$0B` only; NMS and HRM Table 2 give SAX as `$83, $87, $8F, $97` and ANC as `$0B, $2B`. HRM 3.5 SHA leaves out the +1 that NMS and SST give (OI-042). OI-042 asks which source a requirement follows in these cases.

## Options

1. Report them.
2. Only record them here.

## Recommendation

The requirements already follow the correct values. Reporting is your choice.

## Decision

Not decided.
