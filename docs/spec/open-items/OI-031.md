# OI-031: POKEY choices where the HRM conflicts with itself

| Field | Value |

## Question

Are the author's choices for these HRM conflicts accepted?

## Context

`SERIN` read: HRM 14.4 says it clears the data-ready state, HRM 5.6 says it has no side effects; the author chose 5.6. `ALLPOT` polarity: HRM 7.2 against HRM 5.9 and 14.4; the author chose 5.9 and 14.4. Noise generators: HRM 5.5 says XNOR, HRM 14.4 says XOR; the author's model reproduces the 511-entry `RANDOM` table of HRM 14.4. Other choices: the low timer of a linked pair sets its own `IRQST` bit; a 5-bit noise level of 1 passes a clock pulse; the 5-bit audio bit is 1 during init mode.

## Options

1. Accept all choices.
2. Change one of them (state which).

## Recommendation

Option 1. The `RANDOM` table match is strong evidence for the noise model.

## Decision

Not decided.
