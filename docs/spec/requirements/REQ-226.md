# REQ-226: CA2 and CB2 input modes

## Requirement

When control register bits 5-3 are `0xx`, the emulator shall set bit 6 on a CA2 or CB2 transition in the direction that bit 4 selects (0 = negative, 1 = positive), and enable the interrupt when bit 3 = 1.

## Rationale

The CA2 and CB2 lines can issue an IRQ to the CPU (HRM 2.5). In input mode, the line is passively pulled up to the high state (HRM 2.5, footnote 2).

## Acceptance criteria

1. `PACTL` = `$04` (bits 5-3 = `000`). CA2 goes from high to low. `PACTL` bit 6 = 1, and the PIA does not assert IRQ.
2. `PACTL` = `$1C` (bits 5-3 = `011`). CA2 goes from high to low: `PACTL` bit 6 stays 0. CA2 then goes from low to high: `PACTL` bit 6 = 1, and the PIA asserts IRQ.
3. `PBCTL` = `$0C` (bits 5-3 = `001`). CB2 goes from high to low. `PBCTL` bit 6 = 1, and the PIA asserts IRQ.
4. `PACTL` = `$04` and no device drives CA2: the CA2 level is high. `PBCTL` = `$04` and no device drives CB2: the CB2 level is high.
