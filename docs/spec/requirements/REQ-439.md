# REQ-439: Joystick directions

## Requirement

The emulator shall connect the direction switches of joystick port 1 to PIA port A bits 0-3 and of port 2 to bits 4-7 (up, down, left, right from the low bit), with 0 for a closed switch and with no filter for opposite directions.

## Rationale

Games read the joysticks through `PORTA` (`$D300`). The 65XE has two joystick ports.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With both joysticks centered, `PORTA` reads `$FF`.
2. Port 1 up gives `$FE`, port 1 down gives `$FD`, port 1 left gives `$FB` and port 1 right gives `$F7`.
3. Port 2 up gives `$EF`, port 2 down gives `$DF`, port 2 left gives `$BF` and port 2 right gives `$7F`.
4. Port 2 left and right together give `$3F`.

## Notes

The PIA must be set for input on port A, as the OS does at boot (system requirements, REQ-2xx). Ports 3 and 4 of the 400/800 do not exist on the 65XE.
