# REQ-641: Tape motor control

## Requirement

The emulator shall move the inserted tape only in the machine cycles that follow a cycle at whose end the SIO motor line (PIA CA2) is low, shall keep the tape position while the motor line is high, and shall keep the tape position at the RESET key.

## Rationale

The OS starts and stops the cassette motor with the motor line. It stops the motor after each record in the normal inter-record gap mode, and it starts the motor again for the next record. If the tape moved while the motor is off, the OS would lose the data.

## Acceptance criteria

The tape position is the tape time in machine cycles from the start of the tape. It is 0 after the tape is inserted. "The motor line goes low at the end of cycle M" means that the PIA CA2 output changes from high to low in cycle M (REQ-227). The tape signal at tape time p is the level of REQ-642.

1. With a tape inserted and the motor line high, SIO DATA IN is 1 on each of 100,000 cycles, and the tape position stays 0.
2. The motor line goes low at the end of cycle M. On each cycle M + 1 + p, for p from 0 to 200,000, SIO DATA IN is the tape signal at tape time p.
3. Then the motor line goes high at the end of cycle H. SIO DATA IN is 1 on each cycle from H + 1 while the line stays high, and the tape position is H - M. The motor line goes low again at the end of cycle M2. On each cycle M2 + 1 + k, for k from 0 to 200,000, SIO DATA IN is the tape signal at tape time H - M + k.
4. A machine test writes `$34` to `PACTL` in cycle M, and `$3C` to `PACTL` 100,000 cycles later. The tape position is then 100,000, within 2 cycles.
5. A press of the RESET key does not change the tape position. A cold reset (power-on) sets the tape position to 0, and the tape stays inserted.
6. After the eject of the tape, SIO DATA IN is 1 on each cycle, also while the motor line is low.

## Notes

HRM 9.1: "The CA2 control line connects to the motor control line on the SIO bus to activate a cassette tape recorder. It is also active low and enables the cassette motor when lowered." HRM 2.5 gives the same: "the motor line is pulled low when a cassette tape deck should begin recording or playback". TRN OS-APP-K/ATARI-410-TM-Program-Recorder: "The only control the computer has over tape motion is motor start/stop". TRN SRC-CASORG and SRC-SIOORG write `$34` (`MOTRGO`) to `PACTL` to start the motor and `$3C` (`MOTRST`) to stop it.

HRM 9.17 says that the Play or Record button must also be pressed on the 410. The emulated deck always has Play pressed while a tape is inserted. A real deck needs some time to start and stop the tape. The emulated deck starts and stops at once. Both are project decisions.

HRM 9.17 also says that the motor line is the voltage source of the audio track. The emulated tape has no audio track (REQ-639). SIO DATA IN is 1 (mark) while the motor is off (criteria 1 and 3). This is a project decision: the HRM does not give the level of the deck output while the motor is off.

Criterion 5 (the tape goes back to the start at a cold reset) is the recommended option of OI-052, which is open. A real tape does not move back when the computer is switched off.
