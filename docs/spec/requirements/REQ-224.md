# REQ-224: PIA control registers

## Requirement

The emulator shall store bits 0-5 of a write to `PACTL` or `PBCTL`, ignore bits 6 and 7 of the write, and return the interrupt status in bits 7 and 6 of a read.

## Rationale

Bits 7 and 6 show the CA1/CB1 and CA2/CB2 interrupt status. They are read-only (HRM 2.5). The OS writes `$34` and `$3C` to these registers.

## Acceptance criteria

1. No pending interrupt. A write of `$FF` to `PACTL` and then a read of `PACTL` give `$3F`.
2. `PBCTL` bit 7 = 1 (after a CB1 transition). A write of `$3C` to `PBCTL` and then a read of `PBCTL` give `$BC`.
3. `PACTL` = `$3C`. After a CA1 high-to-low transition, a read of `PACTL` returns `$BC`.
4. `PACTL` = `$04` (CA2 input, negative edge). After a CA2 high-to-low transition, a read of `PACTL` returns `$44`.
5. No pending interrupt. A write of `$C0` to `PACTL` and then a read of `PACTL` give `$00`.
