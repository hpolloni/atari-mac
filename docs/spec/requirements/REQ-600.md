# REQ-600: Standard cartridge mapping

## Requirement

The emulator shall map a standard 8 KB cartridge to `$A000-$BFFF` and a standard 16 KB cartridge to `$8000-$BFFF`.

## Rationale

Most cartridges are standard 8 KB or 16 KB cartridges. The left cartridge window is `$A000-$BFFF` and the right cartridge window is `$8000-$9FFF`. A cartridge in the left slot can use both windows.

## Acceptance criteria

The test images use the offset pattern: image byte `i` holds `(i >> 8) & $FF`. All reads are CPU reads through the machine memory map.

1. With an 8 KB cartridge, a read of `$A100` gives `$01`, and a read of `$BFFF` gives `$1F`.
2. With an 8 KB cartridge, a CPU write of `$5A` to `$8000` and then a read of `$8000` gives `$5A` (RAM).
3. With a 16 KB cartridge, a read of `$8100` gives `$01`, a read of `$9FFF` gives `$1F`, and a read of `$A000` gives `$20`.
4. With a 16 KB cartridge, a read of `$BFFF` gives `$3F`.

## Notes

The cartridge asserts RD4 when it maps `$8000-$9FFF` and RD5 when it maps `$A000-$BFFF`. The HRM states that RD5 is active high and signals cartridge ROM in `$A000-$BFFF`.

The image layout (the first 8 KB of a 16 KB image at `$8000`) is a project decision. It is the usual layout of raw and CAR images.
