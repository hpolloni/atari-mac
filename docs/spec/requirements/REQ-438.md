# REQ-438: Console speaker

## Requirement

The emulator shall send the state of `CONSOL` bit 3 of the last write to the audio output as the console speaker signal.

## Rationale

The OS makes the key click with `CONSOL` bit 3. On the XL/XE series, the speaker signal goes to the TV sound.

## Acceptance criteria

1. After a write of `$08` to `CONSOL`, the GTIA speaker state is 1. After a write of `$00`, it is 0.
2. After a write of `$08` and then a write of `$0F` (bit 3 = 1 in both), the GTIA speaker state is 1. After a write of `$00` and then a write of `$07` (bit 3 = 0 in both), it is 0.
3. On the full machine, after boot to the BASIC `READY` prompt with all POKEY channels silent, the audio samples of the 3 frames in which the key of one character is pressed (`type_text("A")`) contain at least two different values.

## Notes

POKEY or the machine mixes the speaker signal into the audio (POKEY requirements, REQ-5xx). The audio level for each speaker state is in those requirements.
