# REQ-322: Jump and wait for vertical blank

## Requirement

The emulator shall execute instruction `$41` (JVB) as a jump that also stops display list fetches until vertical blank, and shall replay the JVB as a one-scan-line blank instruction on each scan line up to scan line 248.

## Rationale

Almost all display lists end with JVB. The display list then repeats each frame without CPU help. Race in Space sets the DLI bit on the JVB (`$C1`) and needs a DLI on every scan line (HRM 4.16).

## Acceptance criteria

1. With `DMACTL` = `$22`, a JVB `$41 $00 $10` on scan line N: `out.dma` is never `DisplayList` or `DisplayListAddress` on scan lines N + 1 to 247.
2. From cycle 8 of scan line N, the display list address in the debug state is `$1000`.
3. On scan line 8 of the next frame, the instruction fetch reads `$1000`.
4. A write of `$00` to `DLISTL` and `$40` to `DLISTH` on scan line N + 5 makes the instruction fetch on scan line 8 of the next frame read `$4000`.
5. With `NMIEN` = `$80`, the instruction `$C1 $00 $10` on scan line N sets `NMIST` bit 7 and causes one NMI on each scan line from N to 247, and causes no DLI on scan lines 248 to the end of the frame and 0-7.
6. A display list at `$1000` is `$42 $00 $30`, 29 × `$02`, `$41 $00 $10` (240 scan lines and the JVB). The test does not write `DLISTL`/`DLISTH` after the start. The frames alternate. In one frame, instruction fetches occur on scan lines 8, 16, ..., 240. In the next frame, the only display list DMA is the JVB on scan line 8 (the fetch of `$41` and its address), and positions `$22-$DD` on scan lines 8-247 have code `%000`.

## Notes

Acceptance criterion 6 is the flicker case in HRM 4.6. Vertical blank cuts off the JVB in the first frame, so the JVB executes at scan line 8 of the next frame.
