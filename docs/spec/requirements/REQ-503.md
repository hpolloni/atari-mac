# REQ-503: Audio clock selection

## Requirement

The emulator shall clock all timers with the 64 kHz clock (machine clock divided by 28) when `AUDCTL` bit 0 is 0, or the 15 kHz clock (divided by 114) when it is 1. It shall clock timer 1 with the machine clock when `AUDCTL` bit 6 is 1, and timer 3 when bit 5 is 1.

## Rationale

All POKEY sound and timer IRQs use these clocks. The pitch of all music depends on them.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With `AUDCTL` = `$00` and `AUDF1` = `$00`, the fires of timer 1 are 28 cycles apart. With `AUDCTL` = `$01` and `AUDF1` = `$00`, they are 114 cycles apart.
2. With `AUDCTL` = `$60` and `AUDF1-4` = `$00`, the fires of timers 1 and 3 are 4 cycles apart, and the fires of timers 2 and 4 are 28 cycles apart. With `AUDCTL` = `$61`, the fires of timers 2 and 4 are 114 cycles apart.
3. With `AUDCTL` = `$40` and `AUDF3` = `$00`, the fires of timer 3 are 28 cycles apart. With `AUDCTL` = `$20` and `AUDF1` = `$00`, the fires of timer 1 are 28 cycles apart.
4. On NTSC, `AUDCTL` = `$00`, `AUDF1` = `$48` and `AUDC1` = `$AF` make the channel 1 digital output a square wave with a period of 4088 cycles (2 x 73 x 28), with 2044 cycles at each level. At 48000 Hz, the measured frequency (see Notes) of the audio output is 437.8 Hz +/- 0.5 Hz.
5. On PAL, the same register values give the same 4088-cycle period. The measured frequency is 433.8 Hz +/- 0.5 Hz.

## Notes

The machine clock is 1.789773 MHz on NTSC and 1.773447 MHz on PAL (HRM 3.3 Cycle timing). The 64 kHz clock is 63.920 kHz (NTSC) or 63.337 kHz (PAL). The 15 kHz clock is 15.700 kHz (NTSC) or 15.557 kHz (PAL).

The 15 kHz clock has no connection to the ANTIC scanline counter. Its phase comes only from the exit from initialization mode (REQ-506).

The fires of a timer are defined in REQ-504 Notes.

Measured frequency. The test takes the samples from 0.5 s to 2.5 s after the register writes (96000 samples at 48000 Hz). It applies a Hann window and computes the magnitude spectrum (bin width 0.5 Hz). The measured frequency is the frequency of the largest peak in the search band, with parabolic interpolation over the peak bin and its two neighbors. The search band is 0.5 to 1.5 times the expected frequency. REQ-511, REQ-533 and REQ-539 use the same method.

POKEY-SPEC 2 and TRN HW-III.F/AUDCTL say that the exact NTSC input frequencies are 1.78979 MHz, 63.9210 kHz and 15.6999 kHz. The HRM says that the NTSC machine clock is 1.789773 MHz (HRM 3.3), which gives 63.920 kHz and 15.700 kHz. This requirement follows the HRM.
