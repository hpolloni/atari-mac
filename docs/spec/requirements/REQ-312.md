# REQ-312: Mode line height and DMA on each scan line

## Requirement

The emulator shall give each playfield mode line the height in the table below, fetch character names and bitmap data only on the first scan line, and fetch character data on every scan line of a character mode line.

| IR mode | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | A | B | C | D | E | F |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scan lines | 8 | 10 | 8 | 16 | 8 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

## Rationale

ANTIC keeps the data of the first scan line in a 48-byte line buffer. It uses the buffer on the next scan lines of the mode line. This reduces DMA.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With `DMACTL` = `$22`, the display list is `$70 $70 $70 $42 $00 $30`, 23 × `$02`, `$41 $00 $10`. Instruction fetches occur on cycle 1 of scan lines 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, ..., 216 (mode 2 lines on scan lines 32-223), and the JVB fetch occurs on scan line 224.
2. For each IR mode 2-F without bit 5, the next instruction fetch occurs H scan lines after the instruction fetch of the mode line. H is the height in the table.
3. In a mode 8 line, `Bitmap` DMA occurs only on the first of its 8 scan lines. The codes of scan lines 2-8 are equal to the codes of scan line 1.
4. In a mode 5 line, `CharacterData` DMA occurs on all 16 scan lines, and `CharacterName` DMA occurs only on the first scan line.
5. In a mode A line, the test changes all 20 screen bytes in memory after the first scan line. The codes of scan lines 2-4 are equal to the codes of scan line 1.
6. For each IR mode 2-F without vertical scrolling, the row counter in the debug state is 0 on the first scan line of the mode line and H - 1 on its last scan line.

## Notes

Vertical scrolling changes the height of some mode lines (REQ-328, REQ-330).
