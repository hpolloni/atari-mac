# REQ-540: Output high-pass stages

## Requirement

The emulator shall filter the POKEY mix with a one-pole high-pass filter with a time constant of 2.6 ms, add the console speaker after that filter, and filter the sum with a one-pole high-pass filter with a time constant of 24.7 ms.

## Rationale

The real audio path removes DC. Without the filters, volume-only playback and stalled channels give a constant offset to the host.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria use NTSC at 48000 Hz and `AUDC1-4` = `$00` unless the criterion gives a different value. The step is on the first cycle of sample number 0. "Peak" is the largest sample from sample 0 to sample 479 (10 ms).

1. With `AUDC1` = `$10` for 1 s, a write of `AUDC1` = `$1F` makes sample 125 (2.6 ms after the step) 25 % to 40 % of the peak.
2. With the speaker level 0 for 1 s, a change of the speaker level to 1 makes sample 1186 (24.7 ms after the step) 33 % to 40 % of the peak.
3. After the step of criterion 1, each sample from 0.5 s to 1.0 s after the step has a magnitude below 0.001.

## Notes

HRM E.3 gives the discrete form `y[n+1] = y[n] + (x[n] - y[n]) * (1 - exp(-1 / (tau * fs)))`. The high-pass output is `x[n] - y[n]`. The HRM also describes clamping in the second stage. The emulator does not emulate the clamping.
