# REQ-430: GTIA mode 10 (9 colors)

## Requirement

With `PRIOR` bits 6-7 = `%10`, the emulator shall map pixel values `%0000-%0011` to the `P0-P3` priority inputs, `%x100-%x111` to `PF0-PF3` and `%10xx` to the background, one color clock later than the other modes.

## Rationale

GRAPHICS 10 shows nine colors from the color registers. The playfield pixels take part in the priority logic like players.

## Acceptance criteria

In all criteria, `COLPM0-3` = `$10`, `$20`, `$30`, `$40`, `COLPF0-3` = `$50`, `$60`, `$70`, `$80` and `COLBK` = `$90`.

1. With `PRIOR` = `$80`, pixel values 0-15 show `$10`, `$20`, `$30`, `$40`, `$50`, `$60`, `$70`, `$80`, `$90`, `$90`, `$90`, `$90`, `$50`, `$60`, `$70`, `$80`.
2. With `PRIOR` = `$80` and a normal width mode F line of `$44` bytes (pixel value 4 at each pixel), HPOS `$30` shows `$10`, HPOS `$31-$D0` show `$50` and HPOS `$D1` shows `$10`. The pixel of HPOS `$30-$31` shows at HPOS `$31-$32`, and the last pixel (HPOS `$CE-$CF`) shows at HPOS `$CF-$D0`.
3. With `PRIOR` = `$80`, the border of a normal width line shows `$10` at HPOS `$23-$2F` and `$D1-$DD`.
4. With `PRIOR` = `$81`, player 1 in the border shows `$10`. With `PRIOR` = `$A1`, player 1 in the border shows `$30` (`$10` OR `$20`).
5. With `PRIOR` = `$80`, player 0 over a pixel with value 5 sets `P0PF` = `$02`. Player 0 over a pixel with value 1 sets `P0PL` = `$00`.
6. With `PRIOR` = `$80` and a wide mode F line of `$44` bytes, HPOS `$2C` shows the delayed border pixel `$10` and HPOS `$2D-$DD` show `$50`. The right edge of the playfield is at HPOS `$DD`, the same as in the other modes.
7. A scanline of `$77` bytes shows with `PRIOR` = `$80`. On the next scanline, a wide mode F line of `$44` bytes, `PRIOR` is `$00` from its horizontal blank, and a write of `$80` to `PRIOR` on cycle 65 (the change takes place at HPOS `$83-$85`) makes HPOS `$88-$DD` show the same colors as a line with `PRIOR` = `$80` from its start. HPOS `$83-$87` do not show the color of pixel value 7 (`COLPF3`) from the scanline before.

## Notes

The HRM reports that some GTIA chips shift mode 10 by one color clock when warm. The emulator does not model this. Criterion 3 starts at HPOS `$23`, because HPOS `$22` shows the delayed pixel of HPOS `$20-$21`, and the codes at HPOS `$20-$21` depend on the ANTIC-to-GTIA interface, which is not settled.

GTIA-SPEC 3.0 says that in the 9 color-lum mode the ANx code `%000` gives the background color. The HRM says that GTIA ignores AN2 and shows the border, where both codes are `%000`, as pixel value `%0000` (the `P0` color, criterion 3). This requirement follows the HRM.

ACID800 gtia_collision2 puts each missile one color clock to the right of its mode 10 pixel and expects the collision with that pixel. Thus the delay of one color clock also applies to the collisions.
