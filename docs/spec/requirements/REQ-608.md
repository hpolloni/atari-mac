# REQ-608: Atarimax bank switching

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Draft |
| Priority | Could |
| Area | Media |
| Source | HRM 8.2 Atarimax flash cartridges |
| Verification | Test |
| Depends on | REQ-605 |

## Requirement

The emulator shall do the bank switching of the MaxFlash 1Mbit and MaxFlash 8Mbit cartridges as the table gives.

| Cartridge | Size | Window | Control range | Bank select | Disable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MaxFlash 1Mbit | 128 KB, 16 banks of 8 KB | `$A000-$BFFF` | Read or write of `$D500-$D51F` | Address bits 0-3 | Address bit 4 = 1 |
| MaxFlash 8Mbit | 1 MB, 128 banks of 8 KB | `$A000-$BFFF` | Read or write of `$D500-$D5FF` | Address bits 0-6 | Address bit 7 = 1 |

## Rationale

These cartridges are a common format for large games and for multi-game images. They are the bank-switched types that the HRM describes. HRM 8.1 does not describe a bank-switched type.

## Acceptance criteria

The test images use the bank pattern: each byte of bank `k` holds `k`. "Does not map" means that RD5 (or RD4) is negated and the cartridge gives no byte for the address.

1. MaxFlash 1Mbit: after a read of `$D503`, a read of `$A000` gives `$03` and a read of `$BFFF` gives `$03`.
2. MaxFlash 1Mbit: after a write of `$00` to `$D50F`, a read of `$A000` gives `$0F`. After a write of `$FF` to `$D502`, a read of `$A000` gives `$02`.
3. MaxFlash 1Mbit: after an access to `$D510`, the cartridge does not map `$A000-$BFFF`.
4. MaxFlash 1Mbit: after an access to `$D510` and then a read of `$D503`, a read of `$A000` gives `$03` (enabled again).
5. MaxFlash 1Mbit: after a read of `$D503`, accesses to `$D520`, `$D55F` and `$D5FF` do not change the result: a read of `$A000` gives `$03`.
6. MaxFlash 8Mbit: after a read of `$D57F`, a read of `$A000` gives `$7F`. After a read of `$D540`, a read of `$A000` gives `$40`.
7. MaxFlash 8Mbit: after an access to `$D580`, the cartridge does not map `$A000-$BFFF`. After an access to `$D5FF`, the cartridge does not map `$A000-$BFFF`.
8. With each cartridge, in any bank state, the cartridge does not map `$8000` or `$9FFF` (RD4 negated).
9. MaxFlash 1Mbit: after a read of `$D503`, a CPU write of `$00` to `$A000` does not change the flash data: a read of `$A000` gives `$03`.

## Notes

The HRM states that the cartridge ignores the data of a write. Flash programming is out of scope: a write to `$A000-$BFFF` does not change the flash data.

The MaxFlash 1Mbit + MyIDE variant and other bank-switched types (for example OSS, XEGS and Williams) are out of scope until a source describes them.
