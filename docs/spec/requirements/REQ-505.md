# REQ-505: Linked 16-bit timers

## Requirement

When `AUDCTL` bit 4 (or bit 3) is 1, the emulator shall link timers 1 and 2 (or 3 and 4) into one 16-bit timer with N = `AUDF1` + 256 x `AUDF2` (or `AUDF3` + 256 x `AUDF4`). The period shall be N + 7 cycles on the 1.79 MHz clock, or (N + 1) x 28 or (N + 1) x 114 cycles on a slow clock.

## Rationale

The OS SIO code uses timers 3 and 4, linked at 1.79 MHz, as the serial clock. Music players use linked timers for exact pitch.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With `AUDCTL` = `$28`, `AUDF3` = `$28` and `AUDF4` = `$00`, the fires of timer 4 are 47 cycles apart.
2. With `AUDCTL` = `$28`, `AUDF3` = `$CC` and `AUDF4` = `$05`, the fires of timer 4 are 1491 cycles apart.
3. With `AUDCTL` = `$10`, `AUDF1` = `$00` and `AUDF2` = `$01`, the fires of timer 2 are 257 x 28 = 7196 cycles apart. With `AUDCTL` = `$09`, `AUDF3` = `$00` and `AUDF4` = `$01`, the fires of timer 4 are 257 x 114 = 29298 cycles apart.
4. With `AUDCTL` = `$50`, `AUDF1` = `$40` and `AUDF2` = `$01`, timer 1 fires exactly 2 times between two consecutive fires of timer 2.
5. With `AUDCTL` = `$28`, `AUDF3` = `$28` and `AUDF4` = `$01`, timer 3 fires 2 times in each period of timer 4, and each fire of timer 4 is 3 cycles after the second fire of timer 3.
6. With the setup of criterion 1, the fires of timer 4 are 47 cycles apart for `AUDC3` = `$00`, `$0F`, `$1F`, `$8F` and `$AF`.

## Notes

The low timer does not reload on its own underflow. It counts through 256 values after its first underflow. When the high timer underflows, both counters reload. The reload does not make the low timer fire again. So the low timer fires `AUDF2` + 1 (or `AUDF4` + 1) times for each fire of the high timer.

The audio output of the low channel stays active. Software usually sets its volume to 0.
