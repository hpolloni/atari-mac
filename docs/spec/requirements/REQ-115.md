# REQ-115: IRQ input

## Requirement

The emulator shall treat IRQ as level triggered and masked by `I`, and shall service it with a 7-cycle sequence that pushes `PC` and `P`, sets `I`, and loads `PC` from `$FFFE`.

## Rationale

POKEY and the PIA share the IRQ line (HRM 5.7). The IRQ handler executes again until all sources are cleared. An IRQ that is removed before `I` is cleared is not serviced (HRM 3.4).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Start: `PC` = `$0200`, `NOP` at `$0200` and `$0201`, `I` = 0, `P` = `$20`, `S` = `$FF`, `$FFFE` = `$00`, `$FFFF` = `$30`. The IRQ line is asserted in cycle 1 (the first cycle of the `NOP` at `$0200`) and stays asserted. The `NOP` runs in cycles 1 and 2. The IRQ sequence runs in cycles 3 to 9. It writes `$02` to `$01FF`, `$01` to `$01FE` and `$20` to `$01FD`. After it, `PC` = `$3000`, `S` = `$FC` and `I` = 1.
2. In criterion 1, cycles 3 and 4 read `$0201`, cycles 5 to 7 are the 3 stack writes, cycle 8 reads `$FFFE` and cycle 9 reads `$FFFF`. Cycle 10 is the opcode fetch at `$3000`.
3. Start: `I` = 1, `NOP` instructions. The IRQ line is asserted for 100 cycles. No interrupt sequence starts: the CPU makes no write.
4. Start: `I` = 1, `NOP` instructions. The IRQ line is asserted for 20 cycles and then released. Then `CLI` and 10 `NOP` instructions execute. No interrupt sequence starts.
5. Start: as in criterion 1, but the handler at `$3000` is one `RTI`. The IRQ line stays asserted. In cycles 3 to 41, the CPU runs the IRQ sequence and the `RTI` 3 times (13 cycles for each pair). The `NOP` at `$0201` does not execute.

## Notes

The HRM gives the 7-cycle length (HRM 3.4). It does not give the IRQ vector address directly. It states that the IRQ and `BRK` share a vector (HRM 3.4 Overlapping interrupts), and the SingleStepTests `BRK` data uses `$FFFE`.
