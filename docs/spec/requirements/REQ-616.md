# REQ-616: SIO checksum

## Requirement

The emulator shall calculate the SIO checksum of a command frame or a data frame as the 8-bit sum of its bytes, with each carry out of bit 7 added back into the sum.

## Rationale

All command frames and data frames end with this checksum. The HRM calls it a carry wrap-around checksum. On the 6502, it is `CLC`, then `ADC` of each byte, then `ADC #0`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The checksum of `31 52 01 00` is `$84`.
2. The checksum of `FF 02` is `$02`.
3. The checksum of `FF FF` is `$FF`.
4. The checksum of 128 bytes of `$FF` is `$FF`.
5. The checksum of 128 bytes of `$00` is `$00`.
6. The checksum of an empty frame is `$00`.
