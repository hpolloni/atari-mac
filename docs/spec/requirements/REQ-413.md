# REQ-413: Player and missile graphics data and colors

## Requirement

The emulator shall show player n from the bits of `GRAFPn` and missile n from bits `2n+1` and `2n` of `GRAFM`, the most significant bit at the left, in the color `COLPMn`.

## Rationale

Four players of 8 bits and four missiles of 2 bits are the sprites of the machine. Each missile shares the color register of the player with the same number.

## Acceptance criteria

In all criteria, `COLBK` = `$02`, `PRIOR` = `$00`, no playfield and P/M DMA off, except where the criterion states other values.

1. With `GRAFP0` = `$81`, `HPOSP0` = `$80`, `SIZEP0` = `$00` and `COLPM0` = `$46`, HPOS `$80` and `$87` show `$46` and HPOS `$81-$86` show `$02`.
2. With `GRAFM` = `$0C`, `HPOSM1` = `$90`, `SIZEM` = `$00` and `COLPM1` = `$94`, HPOS `$90` and `$91` show `$94` and HPOS `$92` shows `$02`.
3. With `GRAFM` = `$02`, `HPOSM0` = `$A0`, `SIZEM` = `$00` and `COLPM0` = `$46`, HPOS `$A0` shows `$46` and HPOS `$A1` shows `$02`.
4. With `COLPM1` = `$16`, `COLPM2` = `$26`, `COLPM3` = `$36`, `GRAFP1-3` = `$80`, `HPOSP1-3` = `$50`, `$60`, `$70`, `GRAFM` = `$F0` and `HPOSM2-3` = `$A0`, `$B0`, HPOS `$50`, `$60`, `$70` show `$16`, `$26`, `$36`, HPOS `$A0-$A1` show `$26` and HPOS `$B0-$B1` show `$36`.
5. With P/M DMA off, `HPOSP0` = `$80`, `COLPM0` = `$46` and one write of `$80` to `GRAFP0` before scanline 8, columns 192-193 of all 240 rows are `$46`.
