# REQ-539: Audio mixing and console speaker

## Requirement

The emulator shall mix the four channel levels and the console speaker linearly, with a mix level of 60 equal to 1.0 and the speaker equal to 0.25, and shall average the mix over the time span of each output sample.

## Rationale

All sound goes through this mix. The OS key click and some programs use the console speaker (GTIA `CONSOL` bit 3).

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria use NTSC at 48000 Hz, with `AUDC1-4` = `$00` unless the criterion gives a different value. "Peak-to-peak" is the maximum minus the minimum of the samples from 1.0 s to 1.1 s after the register writes.

1. `AUDCTL` = `$00`, `AUDF3` = `$07` and `AUDC3` = `$AF` give a measured frequency (REQ-503 Notes) of 3995 Hz +/- 2 Hz and a peak-to-peak of 0.23 to 0.28.
2. The setup of criterion 1 with `AUDC3` = `$A8` gives a peak-to-peak of 8/15 of the value of criterion 1, +/- 5 %.
3. The speaker level changing every 895 cycles (a 1000 Hz square wave) gives a peak-to-peak of 0.23 to 0.28.
4. `AUDCTL` = `$20`, `AUDF3` = `$00` and `AUDC3` = `$AF` (a 224 kHz tone) give a peak-to-peak of less than 0.03.
5. With `AUDC3` = `$10` for 1 s, a write of `AUDC3` = `$1F` makes the first sample that starts after the write larger than the last sample before the write, by 0.20 to 0.26.

## Notes

The HRM gives no absolute level and no level for the console speaker. The values 1.0 and 0.25 are project decisions. The speaker value is the same as one channel at volume 15.

HRM E.4 states that the console speaker joins the POKEY signal after the first amplifier stage (REQ-540). The speaker input is the level of the `CONSOL` bit 3 line from GTIA. The GTIA teammate specifies that register.

Criteria 1-3 assume the linear mix. REQ-541 is an option that changes the levels.
