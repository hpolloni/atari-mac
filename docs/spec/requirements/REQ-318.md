# REQ-318: Mapped modes 8 to F

## Requirement

The emulator shall show the bitmap data of IR modes 8 to F with the pixel sizes and color encodings in the table below, with bit 7 of each byte as the leftmost pixel.

| IR mode | Bits per pixel | Pixel width | Colors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | 2 | 4 color clocks | `BAK`, `PF0`, `PF1`, `PF2` |
| 9 | 1 | 2 color clocks | `BAK`, `PF0` |
| A | 2 | 2 color clocks | `BAK`, `PF0`, `PF1`, `PF2` |
| B, C | 1 | 1 color clock | `BAK`, `PF0` |
| D, E | 2 | 1 color clock | `BAK`, `PF0`, `PF1`, `PF2` |
| F | 1 | 1/2 color clock (hi-res) | Hi-res pixel bits |

## Rationale

The OS Graphics 3 to Graphics 15 modes use these modes. Mode F is also the base of the GTIA modes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. In modes 8, A, D and E, a byte `$1B` gives the codes `%000`, `%100`, `%101` and `%110`, left to right. Each pixel is 4 color clocks wide in mode 8, 2 in mode A, and 1 in modes D and E.
2. In modes 9, B and C, a byte `$55` gives the codes `%000` and `%100` in turn, starting with `%000`. Each pixel is 2 color clocks wide in mode 9 and 1 in modes B and C.
3. In mode F, a byte `$1B` gives the codes `%100`, `%101`, `%110` and `%111`, each for 1 color clock (REQ-307).
4. A normal-width line fetches 10 bytes in modes 8 and 9, 20 bytes in modes A to C, and 40 bytes in modes D to F. Byte k starts at position `$30 + 16k` (modes 8, 9), `$30 + 8k` (modes A-C) or `$30 + 4k` (modes D-F).
