# REQ-105: Zero page wrap and JMP indirect page bug

## Requirement

The emulator shall keep zero page indexed addresses and zero page pointers in page zero, and shall fetch the high byte of a `JMP ($xxFF)` target from `$xx00`.

## Rationale

The zero page modes wrap from `$00FF` to `$0000` (HRM 3.3). The NMOS 6502 has the well-known `JMP (abs)` bug, which accesses `$xxFF` and `$xx00`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Start: `X` = `$20`. `LDA $F0,X` reads the operand at `$0010`, not at `$0110`.
2. Start: `Y` = `$20`. `LDX $F0,Y` reads the operand at `$0010`.
3. Start: `X` = `$0F`. `LDA ($F0,X)` reads the pointer low byte at `$00FF` and the pointer high byte at `$0000`.
4. Start: `Y` = `$00`, `$00FF` = `$34`, `$0000` = `$12`, `$0100` = `$56`. `LDA ($FF),Y` reads the pointer low byte at `$00FF` and the pointer high byte at `$0000`. It reads the operand at `$1234`.
5. Start: `$30FF` = `$00`, `$3000` = `$40`, `$3100` = `$50`. `JMP ($30FF)` reads `$30FF` and `$3000`, and gives `PC` = `$4000`.

## Notes

The HRM states the wrap for `zp,X`, `zp,Y` and `(zp,X)`. NMS zeropage-page-wrap also states the wrap of the `(zp),Y` pointer read at `$FF`: `LDA ($FF),Y` reads the low byte at `$00FF` and the high byte at `$0000`. The SingleStepTests 6502 data agrees.

MCS6500-HW App-A (A.4.3) and MCS6500-PRG 6.2 show the zero page index addition without carry. NMS indirect-page-wrap states the `JMP ($xxFF)` bug. ACID800 cpu_insn fails when `zp,X`, `zp,Y` and `(zp,X)` do not wrap, and ACID800 cpu_bugs fails when the `JMP (abs)` bug is not present.
