# REQ-109: Scope of the decimal flag

## Requirement

The emulator shall apply the `D` flag only to `ADC`, `SBC` (also opcode `$EB`), `ISB`, `RRA` and `ARR`, and shall not change `D` on reset or on interrupt entry.

## Rationale

`CMP`, `CPX`, `CPY`, `INC`, `DEC` and indexing are not affected by `D` (HRM 3.1). NMOS 6502s do not clear `D` automatically, so reset code and interrupt handlers must clear it (HRM 3.1, HRM 3.6).

## Acceptance criteria

1. For `CMP #imm`, `CPX #imm`, `CPY #imm`, `INC zp`, `DEC zp`, `INX`, `DEX`, `INY` and `DEY`, `A`, `X`, `Y` and the operand each take the values `$00`, `$09`, `$0F`, `$10`, `$99` and `$FF`. For each combination, a run with `D` = 1 gives the same `A`, `X`, `Y`, memory and flags (except `D`) as a run with `D` = 0.
2. All SingleStepTests 6502 cases with `D` = 1 in the files of `ISB` (`e3`, `e7`, `ef`, `f3`, `f7`, `fb`, `ff`), `RRA` (`63`, `67`, `6f`, `73`, `77`, `7b`, `7f`) and `ARR` (`6b`) agree with the file.
3. Start: `D` = 1, `I` = 0. After an IRQ sequence, after an NMI sequence and after a `BRK` sequence, `D` = 1. The pushed `P` has bit 3 = 1.
4. Start: `D` = 1. After the reset sequence, `D` = 1. Start: `D` = 0. After the reset sequence, `D` = 0.

## Notes

HRM 3.5 says that `ISB` and `RRA` are sensitive to `D`, and that `ARR` does a possible decimal correction. The exact results of `ARR` follow the SingleStepTests data (REQ-111).
