# REQ-051: Evidence for inspection and demonstration

## Requirement

When the work package of a requirement with the verification method `inspection` or `demonstration` is `Done`, an evidence record `docs/verification/REQ-NNN.md` shall exist and shall record a passed result.

## Rationale

Without a record, nobody can tell whether a manual check was done.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The record has frontmatter with `requirement`, `date` (ISO 8601), `commit`, `method`, `result` (`pass` or `fail`) and `by`.
2. The body gives the steps and what was seen, for each acceptance criterion of the requirement.
3. For a `Done` package, a missing record or a record with `result: fail` makes the check fail.

## Notes

Project decision: the specification gates are a project rule. The HRM does not cover them.
