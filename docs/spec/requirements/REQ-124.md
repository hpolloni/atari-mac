# REQ-124: CPU reset sequence

## Requirement

The emulator shall run a 7-cycle reset sequence that sets `I`, keeps `D`, decrements `S` by 3 without writes, and loads `PC` from `$FFFC`.

## Rationale

The OS starts at the reset vector after power-on, and after the Reset button on the XL/XE (HRM 2.4).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Start: `S` = `$FF`, `$FFFC` = `$00`, `$FFFD` = `$C0`. After `reset`, the CPU makes 7 reads and no write. Cycle 6 reads `$FFFC` and cycle 7 reads `$FFFD`. Cycle 8 is the opcode fetch at `$C000`.
2. After the reset sequence of criterion 1, `S` = `$FC`. Start: `S` = `$01`. After the reset sequence, `S` = `$FE`.
3. After the reset sequence, `I` = 1. Start: `D` = 1. After the reset sequence, `D` = 1. Start: `D` = 0. After the reset sequence, `D` = 0.
4. The CPU executes a KIL opcode (REQ-113). After `reset`, the CPU runs the reset sequence and then executes the instruction at the reset vector address.
5. The NMI line goes high in the cycle before `reset` and stays high. After the reset sequence, the CPU makes no read of `$FFFA` in the next 1,000 cycles.

## Notes

The HRM states that the I flag is set on reset and that the NMOS 6502 does not clear D (HRM 3.1, HRM 3.6). It gives the reset vector at `$FFFC` (HRM 3.7). The 7-cycle length, the decrement of `S` and the read-only cycles are not in the HRM. MCS6500-PRG 9.2 (Example 9.1) states them: the start sequence takes 7 cycles, cycles 3 to 5 access the stack at `S`, `S` - 1 and `S` - 2 with no writes, cycle 6 reads `$FFFC`, cycle 7 reads `$FFFD`, and cycle 8 fetches the first opcode. NMS fetch-hardware-interrupts and MCS6500-HW 1.4.1.2.11 also say that the CPU does not write during reset. MCS6500-PRG 9.3 says that a reset only sets `I` and loads `PC` from the reset vector. The clear of a pending NMI edge is a project decision.
