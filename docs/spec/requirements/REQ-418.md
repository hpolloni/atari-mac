# REQ-418: P/M retrigger and continuous shifting

## Requirement

The emulator shall load an object's shift register by a bitwise OR with its graphics latch at each color clock where the position matches, and shall shift the register at all color clocks, including horizontal and vertical blank.

## Rationale

Programs move a sprite in the middle of a scanline to show it more than once. The collision registers make this behavior visible to programs (HRM 1.4).

## Acceptance criteria

In all criteria, `GRAFP0` = `$FF`, `SIZEP0` = `$00`, `COLPM0` = `$46` and `COLBK` = `$02`, except where the criterion states other values. "Takes effect at HPOS h" means that the new value applies from color clock h (REQ-434).

1. With `HPOSP0` = `$40` and a write of `$A0` to `HPOSP0` that takes effect at HPOS `$67` (cycle 50), HPOS `$40-$47` and `$A0-$A7` show `$46`, and all other HPOS of `$22-$DD` show `$02`.
2. With `HPOSP0` = `$A0` and a write of `$40` to `HPOSP0` that takes effect at HPOS `$67` (cycle 50), HPOS `$22-$DD` of that scanline show `$02`.
3. With `HPOSP0` = `$60` and a write of `$50` to `HPOSP0` that takes effect at HPOS `$63` (cycle 48), HPOS `$60-$67` show `$46` and HPOS `$68-$DD` show `$02`.
4. With `GRAFP0` = `$A0`, `HPOSP0` = `$60` and a write of `$61` to `HPOSP0` that takes effect at HPOS `$61` (cycle 47), HPOS `$60-$63` show `$46` and HPOS `$64-$DD` show `$02`. The load at `$61` gives the shifted old bits OR the new bits.
5. With `HPOSP0` = `$1A`, HPOS `$22-$DD` show `$02`. The image shifts out in horizontal blank.

## Notes

The HRM states that overlap and lockup effects can carry from vertical blank into the next frame.
