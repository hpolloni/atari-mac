# REQ-537: Truncated and suspended pot scans

## Requirement

The emulator shall continue to charge the pot inputs when `POTGO` restarts a scan before the scan ends, and while initialization mode stops the slow scan counter.

## Rationale

Some programs extend the scan to read the full range of a CX30 paddle.

## Acceptance criteria

In all criteria, the scan is a slow scan with the setup of REQ-534 (first `POTGO` on cycle G).

1. With `set_pot(0, 150)`, a second `POTGO` write on cycle G + 64 x 114 makes `POT0` read 86 +/- 1 after the second scan.
2. With `set_pot(0, 150)`, a write of `SKCTL` = `$00` on cycle G + 20 x 114 and a write of `SKCTL` = `$03` on cycle G + 70 x 114 make `POT0` read 100 +/- 1 after the scan.
3. After the second `POTGO` write of criterion 1 on cycle G2, `ALLPOT` reads `$00` first on cycle P2 + 227 x 114 + 2. P2 is the cycle of the first pot counter increment after G2.
4. With `set_pot(0, 30)`, a second `POTGO` write on cycle G + 64 x 114 keeps `POT0` at 30 and `ALLPOT` bit 0 at 0 on each cycle of the second scan.

## Notes

The emulator models the capacitor charge as a time in cycles. The phase of the 15 kHz clock gives the tolerance of +/- 1. The emulator does not model the voltage curve or the residual charge that HRM 5.9 describes for low counts.
