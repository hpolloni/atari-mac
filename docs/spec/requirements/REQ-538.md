# REQ-538: Audio sample output

## Requirement

The emulator shall make mono `f32` audio samples at the configured host sample rate. After C machine cycles since power-on, the total number of samples shall be the integer part of C x sample rate / machine clock rate.

## Rationale

The frontend plays the samples without gaps (REQ-011). The sample count must follow the emulated time exactly, so that the audio buffer does not drift.

## Acceptance criteria

1. On NTSC at 48000 Hz, 60 frames (1792080 cycles) after `Pokey::new` give exactly 48061 samples (the sum of the lengths that `drain_samples` adds).
2. On PAL at 48000 Hz, 50 frames (1778400 cycles) give exactly 48134 samples.
3. With the input of criterion 4, and with `AUDC1-4` changing between `$1F` and `$10` every 1000 cycles, each sample is in the range -1.0 to 1.0.
4. With `AUDCTL` = `$00`, `AUDF1` = `$48`, `AUDC1` = `$AF` and the speaker level changing every 895 cycles, the samples of one `drain_samples` call after 10 frames are bit-identical to the samples of 10 calls, one after each frame.
5. On NTSC, after 1792080 cycles, the number of samples is 8010 at 8000 Hz, 44156 at 44100 Hz, 96123 at 96000 Hz and 192247 at 192000 Hz.

## Notes

The HRM does not cover host audio output. The machine clock rates are 1789773 Hz (NTSC) and 1773447 Hz (PAL), from HRM 3.3. The rate range 8000 Hz to 192000 Hz is a project decision. The behavior for a rate outside this range is not specified yet.
