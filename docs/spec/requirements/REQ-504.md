# REQ-504: Unlinked timer period

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Draft |
| Priority | Must |
| Area | POKEY: audio, timers, keyboard, interrupts, serial port |
| Source | HRM 5.3 Sound generation (Timer period) |
| Verification | Test |
| Depends on | REQ-503 |

## Requirement

The emulator shall give an unlinked timer with `AUDFx` = N a period of N + 4 cycles on the 1.79 MHz clock, (N + 1) x 28 cycles on the 64 kHz clock and (N + 1) x 114 cycles on the 15 kHz clock.

## Rationale

The period sets the pitch of each channel and the rate of the timer IRQs. Music players and demos depend on the exact period.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With `AUDCTL` = `$40` and `AUDF1` = N, the fires of timer 1 are N + 4 cycles apart, for N = 0, 1, 5 and 255.
2. With `AUDCTL` = `$00` and `AUDF1` = `$09`, the fires of timer 1 are 280 cycles apart.
3. With `AUDCTL` = `$01` and `AUDF4` = `$00`, the fires of timer 4 are 114 cycles apart. With `AUDF4` = `$09`, they are 1140 cycles apart.
4. With `AUDCTL` = `$00`, `AUDF1` = `$03`, `AUDF2` = `$01` and an `STIMER` write, each fire of timer 1 is on the same cycle as a fire of timer 2.

## Notes

A "fire" is the underflow event that clocks the audio circuit, the serial port and the timer IRQ. The cycle of a fire is the cycle on which its IRQ shows in `IRQST` (HRM 1.2 Event timing), also when the IRQ is disabled. Tests read the fire cycles of all four timers through the test accessor of `docs/design/pokey.md`.

The +3 delay of the 1.79 MHz case is hidden for the slow clocks, because the next slow clock pulse occurs 28 or 114 cycles after the previous pulse.

For the common square wave (`AUDCx` = `$Ax`), the frequency of the tone is half of the fire rate.
