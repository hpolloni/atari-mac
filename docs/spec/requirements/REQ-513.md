# REQ-513: 4-bit and 5-bit polynomial counters

## Requirement

The emulator shall step the 4-bit (1 + x^3 + x^4) and 5-bit (1 + x^3 + x^5) polynomial counters once per machine cycle, and shall start both from the zero state when initialization mode ends.

## Rationale

The 4-bit and 5-bit counters make the noise of distortions 0, 2, 4, 6 and 12.

## Acceptance criteria

In criteria 1 and 2, `SKCTL` = `$00` is held for at least 18 cycles, and then `SKCTL` = `$03` is written on cycle W. The test reads the noise bits of channel 1 through the test accessor.

1. The 4-bit noise bits of channel 1 on cycles W+d, W+d+1, W+d+2, ... are `000011101100101`, and then the sequence repeats with a period of 15 cycles. d is 0 or 1.
2. The 5-bit noise bits of channel 1 on cycles W+d, W+d+1, ... are `0000011100100010101111011010011`, and then the sequence repeats with a period of 31 cycles. d is 0 or 1.
3. From 9 cycles after a write of `SKCTL` = `$00` until the next write that ends initialization mode, the 4-bit noise bit of each channel is 0 and the 5-bit noise bit of each channel is 1. With `AUDCTL` = `$40`, `AUDF1` = `$00` and `AUDC1` = `$CF`, the channel 1 flip-flop is 0 after each fire. With `AUDC1` = `$2F`, the channel 1 flip-flop changes on each fire.

## Notes

The HRM gives the 4-bit pattern also as `000111011001010` in HRM 5.3. This is the same cycle, rotated.

The HRM does not state on which cycle after the `SKCTL` write the first pattern bit is output. So criteria 1 and 2 accept d = 0 or d = 1.

HRM 5.5 says "shortly after" for criterion 3. The 9 cycles are a project decision. They are the same as the `RANDOM` delay of REQ-502 criterion 6.

TRN HW-II.B/Poly-Noise-Counters and POKEY-SPEC 2 state that the 1.79 MHz clock steps all polynomial counters.
